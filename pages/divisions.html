Our Businesses

Omnitryx Inc. builds and operates multiple businesses, each one a different way to create value, serve people, and contribute to stronger communities. Below is what we currently run and what is being built.

Active

Technology Division

Enterprise software, cloud infrastructure, and intelligent systems.

The Omnitryx Technology Division helps businesses solve their hardest technical problems. We build and deploy web platforms, mobile applications, API systems, AI-powered automation, and cloud infrastructure. The kind of work that keeps companies running and growing.

This division is led by Jawwad Abbasi, a Senior Software Developer with five years of enterprise experience at CGI and a founder who has built and launched his own production mobile platform. Every engagement is treated with the same standards he applies in his corporate work: no shortcuts, no disappearing, no cutting corners.

Publishing Division

Books and knowledge for people who want to grow.

The Omnitryx Publishing Division produces books authored by Jawwad Abbasi and published through Amazon KDP. The current catalogue focuses on faith, personal growth, and real stories that inspire people to take action in their lives.

Future titles will expand into entrepreneurship, community building, and practical technology. All books are distributed globally through Amazon and available worldwide.

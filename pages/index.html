Omnitryx Inc. is a federally incorporated Canadian company with a clear purpose: to build businesses that create jobs, serve people, and make communities economically stronger. We operate across technology, publishing, and more to come.

Our Businesses Meet the Founder

What Omnitryx Is

Omnitryx Inc. is a multi-business company built on a simple belief: strong businesses create strong communities. When businesses grow, people get hired. When people get hired, families get stronger. When families get stronger, communities thrive.

Omnitryx exists because we believe business, done right, is one of the most powerful ways to improve the world.

How We Think

We run multiple businesses under one company because each one is a different way to serve. The technology division helps businesses grow by solving their hardest technical problems. The publishing division puts knowledge and ideas into the hands of people who need them. Future ventures will open more doors.

Every business we build is guided by the same values: honesty, quality, and genuine care for the people we work with and for.

Founder & Chief Executive

Jawwad Abbasi

Jawwad Abbasi is a Canadian software engineer, entrepreneur, and published author based in Ontario. He spent five years as a Senior Software Developer at CGI, one of Canada's largest technology companies, and has shipped production software used by thousands of people worldwide.

He founded Omnitryx Inc. because he believes the best use of his skills is not just writing code. It is building businesses that employ people, solve real problems, and create opportunity for those who need it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Omnitryx, our businesses, and how we work.

Who runs Omnitryx Inc.?

Omnitryx Inc. is founded and led by Jawwad Abbasi. View his profile on LinkedIn.

Why build multiple businesses instead of one?

Different problems require different solutions. A consulting firm can solve one kind of problem. Omnitryx is built to create businesses wherever there is an opportunity to serve people and create meaningful work, regardless of industry.

Where is Omnitryx based?

Omnitryx Inc. is federally incorporated in Canada and operates out of Ontario. We serve clients across Canada and can work with businesses internationally.

How can I work with the technology division?

Book a 30-minute call or reach out directly at jawwad@omnitryx.ca. Describe what you are working on and we will take it from there.

Are you currently hiring?

Keep an eye on Jawwad's LinkedIn for any updates as Omnitryx grows.

Can we partner with or collaborate with Omnitryx?

Yes. We are open to meaningful partnerships that align with our mission. Email us at contact@omnitryx.ca with your proposal.

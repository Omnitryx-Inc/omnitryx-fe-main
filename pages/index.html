We Build Businesses

Omnitryx Inc. is a federally incorporated Canadian company with a clear purpose: to build businesses that create real jobs, serve real people, and make communities economically stronger. We operate across technology, publishing, and more to come.

Our Businesses Meet the Founder

What Omnitryx Is

Omnitryx Inc. is a multi-business company built on a simple belief: strong businesses create strong communities. When businesses grow, people get hired. When people get hired, families get stronger. When families get stronger, communities thrive.

We did not start Omnitryx to chase profit for its own sake. We built it to create something that matters: businesses that serve people honestly, employ people meaningfully, and leave the world better than they found it.

How We Think

We run multiple businesses under one company because each one is a different way to serve. The technology division helps businesses grow by solving their hardest technical problems. The publishing division puts knowledge and ideas into the hands of people who need them. Future ventures will open more doors.

Every business we build is guided by the same values: honesty, quality, and genuine care for the people we work with and for.

Founder & Chief Executive

Jawwad Abbasi

Jawwad Abbasi is a Canadian software engineer, entrepreneur, and published author based in Ontario. He brings experience from both sides of the industry: large corporations and early-stage startups.

He has spent five years as a Senior Software Developer at CGI, one of Canada's largest technology companies, designing enterprise cloud systems and shipping production software used by major clients. He has also worked in fast-paced startups, putting in the hours to build and launch apps from scratch that went on to be used by thousands of people worldwide.

Jawwad founded Omnitryx Inc. because he believes the best use of his skills is not just writing code. It is building businesses that employ people, solve real problems, and create opportunity for those who need it.
